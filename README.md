📌 About the Project

MoneyMind was developed by Mahlatse Molepo, Dimpho Gfit Mphahlele, Tlhologelo Mmako and Douglas Makofane as part of CMPG 212 Group Project. The brief was to build a mobile app that works on both Android and iOS. Choosing the topic was the easy part. As students we know exactly what it feels like to watch your money disappear before the month is even halfway through, to lose track of what you spent, to owe people and not have a plan to get on top of it. Nobody really teaches you how to manage money you just figure it out the hard way. That experience is what inspired MoneyMind. We wanted to build something that not just students but anyone could use to take back control of their finances, understand where their money is going and actually make a plan for where it should go.
